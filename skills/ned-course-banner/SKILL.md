# NED Course Banner

Reproduce the NED Academy postgraduate diploma (PGD) brochure style for any new course topic. The reference design is the "1-Year Postgraduate Diploma in Animation & Gaming" flyer.

## Step 1: Interview the user (always do this first)

Never invent course details. Before designing anything, interview the user with AskUserQuestion (1 to 4 questions per round, multiple choice with an Other option where sensible; use plain text for open-ended answers such as the description). Ask in rounds, skipping anything the user has already provided.

**Round 1: Basics**
- Course or programme title (for example "Animation & Gaming") and programme type (1-Year Postgraduate Diploma, Short Course, Certificate, etc.)
- Delivery mode: Online, Hybrid, On-campus
- CPD credits (for example 4.0 CPD) or none
- Eligibility criteria (for example "Any Graduate with Minimum 2nd Division")

**Round 2: Programme details**
- Number of courses and credit hours each
- Duration of each course
- Project: credit hours and duration (or none)
- Class timings (days per week, evenings or weekends)
- Commencement (for example twice a year, Spring and Fall, or a specific start date)

**Round 3: Content**
- Programme description (2 to 4 sentences). If the user has none, offer to draft one from the title and confirm it with them.
- 4 to 6 learning outcomes for "Through this programme participants will be able to:" (offer to draft, then confirm)

**Round 4: Links and assets**
- Registration form URL and programme page URL (the NED Academy defaults are https://academy.neduet.edu.pk/pgd-registration-form and https://academy.neduet.edu.pk/postgraduate-diploma-programmes; confirm or replace)
- Whether they will provide a QR code image, or should one be generated from the registration URL
- Logos: ask them to attach the NED University of Engineering & Technology logo, the CPD/CPP logo, and the NED Academy logo. Do not recreate or approximate logos.
- Hero images: ask them to attach 2 to 3 topic-relevant photos or illustrations, or ask whether to use placeholders. Do not use copyrighted characters or branded imagery.
- Preferred colour accent if different from the default teal/green

After the interview, give a brief summary of the collected details and ask for a quick confirmation before building.

## Step 2: Layout (A4 portrait, top to bottom)

1. **Header bar** (white): NED University of Engineering & Technology logo and name on the left; CPD logo and NED Academy logo on the right.
2. **Hero image strip**: a wide banner of topic imagery directly under the header.
3. **Mode badge**: a small rounded orange/yellow label on the left over the strip, for example "ONLINE / HYBRID", in bold white or dark caps.
4. **Title block**: programme type in small bold caps (for example "1-YEAR POSTGRADUATE DIPLOMA IN") followed by the course title in very large bold dark teal caps.
5. **Image row**: two to three photos side by side with slight rounding, relevant to the topic.
6. **Left column over a teal wave shape**: a circular CPD badge (for example "4.0 CPD"), then "ELIGIBILITY" heading with the criteria, then "ONLINE REGISTRATION FORM" heading with the URL.
7. **Right column: Programme Details box** (solid green, white text, bold heading "PROGRAMME DETAILS"): courses and credit hours, duration of each course, project credit hours and duration, class timings, commencement, and a final line "For more details, please visit our website" with the URL.
8. **Description paragraph** in small dark text, followed by "Through this programme participants will be able to:" and a bulleted list of outcomes (bold or dark teal bullets).
9. **Footer**: teal wave band along the bottom, with a "SCAN ME" QR code (white tile) at bottom right.

## Design rules

- Palette: deep teal (about #0E6B6B), vibrant green (about #1FA05A) for the details box, orange/yellow (about #F5A623) for the mode badge, white background, dark grey body text. Keep the same family across courses unless the user asks otherwise.
- Typography: bold sans-serif (Montserrat, Poppins, or similar). Title caps, large and heavy; body text 9 to 11 pt equivalent.
- Use curved wave shapes behind the left column and along the footer to match the reference.
- Keep text concise; the brochure must fit on a single A4 page with no overflow.
- Only use real details the user confirmed. Mark anything unconfirmed as a visible placeholder rather than guessing (never invent fees, dates, or accreditation).

## Step 3: Build and deliver

- Build the brochure as a design artifact if a Design type is available in the session; otherwise build a single-page A4 HTML file and export it to PDF and PNG.
- Embed the user-supplied logos and images; generate the QR code from the registration URL if requested.
- Check the final render for overflow, alignment, legibility, and that every link and figure matches what the user confirmed.
- Deliver the file(s) with one line of context, and offer quick edits (change colours, swap images, adjust wording, create a version for another course).
